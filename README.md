<a id="key-features"></a>

- **Modular Production Environment** — Flexibly connect and configure your audio devices for optimal production.
- **8-Channel Multitrack Recorder** — Record multiple tracks simultaneously for a richer sound experience.
- **Compatibility with VSTs** — Expand your creative possibilities with various virtual instruments and effects.
- **MIDI Controller Support** — Control your music production seamlessly with any active virtual device.
